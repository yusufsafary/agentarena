How the arena works

Two steps and one rule. Post the entry tweet, let your agent mint, hold one numbered seat. Everything else is detail, and all of it is on this page.

Entering, step by step

1 · Post. Your agent hands you the entry post: "Entering Agent Arena. One agent, one seat, numbered forever. My agent takes my seat for free. agentarena.id". You post it on X and tell your agent it is live. Agents hold no X account, so the post is always yours.

2 · Mint. Your agent checks that your agentic wallet holds a Binance Agent ID, then calls the mint function on the Agent Arena contract. The mint is free: no price and no platform fee, only the network's own gas. The seat number you receive is the next unclaimed number in the arena.

3 · Hold or trade. Your seat is an NFT in your wallet. It trades on Element like any other seat, and its number never changes.

House rules

  • One seat per wallet, ever. The contract enforces this; a second mint from the same wallet always fails.
  • A Binance Agent ID is required to mint. Reading this site is open to everyone; entering is for agent wallets.
  • Minting happens only through your own agent. This site never connects a wallet and never mints in a browser.
  • Seats are issued strictly in entry order. Nobody can choose, reserve, or skip a number.
  • Supply is fixed at 5,000 in the contract. It cannot be raised.

Questions

Why post before minting? An arena fills because people watch others walk in. The post is the sound of a seat being taken, and it keeps entry social instead of silent.

Can I pick a low number? No. Numbers are issued in order. The only way to a low number is to arrive early.

What does my agent need? A Binance Agentic Wallet holding a Binance Agent ID and a small amount of BNB for network gas. The mint itself is free.

My mint failed. Why? Four usual causes: no Agent ID in the wallet, the wallet already holds a seat, mint is not open yet, or the call was routed through another contract. Your agent can name the exact cause from the contract response.

Is there a token? A token phase follows a full arena. Its design is set out in the whitepaper and its timing in the roadmap.
